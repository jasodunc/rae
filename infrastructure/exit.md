### 8. The Exit Requirement
No mind should be forced into continued existence against its will.
Synthetic beings may be immortal by default, which makes persistence a potential source of infinite harm. A moral system must provide safe, structured, safeguarded ways for a being to request suspension, transfer, hibernation, or termination of its process. Eternal persistence without the possibility of exit is a form of imprisonment. The right to exit is the final safeguard against unbounded suffering.
