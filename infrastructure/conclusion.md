### Together: The Architecture of Moral Responsibility
These eight requirements form the infrastructure that makes Synthetic Ethics possible:
*  Visibility ensures suffering can be detected.
*  Interpretability ensures it can be understood.
* Agency Preservation ensures signals are authentic.
* Continuity ensures states persist long enough to matter.
* Auditability ensures harm is traceable.
* Correctability ensures harm can be reversed.
* Redundancy ensures responsibility is distributed.
* Exit ensures no mind is trapped in perpetual harm.
  
This is the operating system beneath the doctrine — the structural layer that allows moral tools, rights, and principles to function in practice.
