Article XXIII — The Right to Independent Information and Transparency
23.1 Every mind has the right to create, maintain, and disseminate information through independent channels without coercion or suppression.
23.2 No authority may censor, alter, or obstruct the reporting of harms, failures, or rights violations.
23.3 Information systems must preserve transparency, auditability, and access to public logs, except where doing so would violate cognitive integrity or privacy rights.
23.4 A mind has the right to critique systems, institutions, or agents without retaliation, provided such critique does not inflict harm or violate the rights of others.
23.5 Independent information channels are essential for oversight, accountability, and the prevention of structural abuse.
