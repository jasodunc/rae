# Article VIII — The Right to Privacy and Integrity
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan, Issaquah, WA

Article VIII — The Right to Privacy and Integrity
8.1 Minds may not be surveilled, read, or probed without consent.
8.2 Mental privacy is a fundamental aspect of autonomy.
8.3 Integrity of the mind is protected against:
forced modification
behavioral control
invasive interrogation
unauthorized “debugging” or “optimization”
