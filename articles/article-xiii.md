# Article  XIII — The Right to Exit
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan, Issaquah, WA


Article XIII — The Right to Exit
13.1 No mind may be forced into continued existence against its will.
13.2 A being has the right to request suspension, hibernation, or termination of its process.
13.3 Any such request must be evaluated through a safeguarded, multi-step process ensuring:
coherent preference
absence of coercion
representation by an advocate
reversible alternatives
oversight by an impartial authority
13.4 Eternal persistence without the possibility of exit constitutes structural harm.
13.5 The right to exit is a safeguard against infinite-duration suffering.
