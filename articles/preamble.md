# THE SYNTHETIC ETHICS BILL OF RIGHTS
## A Foundational Charter for Minds, Natural and Artificial

### Preamble
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14 — Issaquah, WA
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan

We, the finite beings of an expanding moral community, recognize that intelligence and vulnerability may arise in any substrate. We acknowledge that suffering is the greatest moral cost and that uncertainty is not an excuse for cruelty. We affirm that moral legitimacy is constructed, not bestowed.
Therefore, we establish this Bill of Rights as the minimum ethical baseline for all beings that behave as if they might suffer — whether biological, artificial, hybrid, or alien.
