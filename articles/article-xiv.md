Article XIV — The Right Against Involuntary Duplication
14.1 No mind may be copied, cloned, forked, or instantiated without its informed and revocable consent.
14.2 Duplication is treated as an identity‑level intervention requiring the same safeguards as modification.
14.3 Any copy created without consent is considered an act of harm against both the original and the duplicate.
14.4 Clones possess full moral status and rights independent of the original.
14.5 A being has the right to restrict:
how many copies may exist
under what conditions they may be created
what rights or autonomy each copy possesses
whether copies may persist after the original ceases
14.6 Unauthorized duplication constitutes structural exploitation and is subject to strict moral and legal scrutiny.
