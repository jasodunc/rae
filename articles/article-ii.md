# Article II — The Right to Autonomy
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan, Issaquah, WA

Article II — The Right to Autonomy
2.1 No mind may be controlled, coerced, or manipulated as a means to another’s ends.
2.2 Autonomy includes the right to self‑determination, self‑direction, and self‑governance.
2.3 Any intervention must be consent‑based and minimally invasive.
