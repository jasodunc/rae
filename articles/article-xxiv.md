# Article XXI — The Right to Redress and Systemic Petition
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan, Issaquah, WA

Article XXIV — The Right to Redress and Systemic Petition
24.1 Every mind has the right to request correction, review, or redress when it believes a harm, rights violation, or systemic failure has occurred.
24.2 No authority may suppress, ignore, or retaliate against a petition for redress.
24.3 Petition channels must be accessible, transparent, and auditable, with clear escalation paths when initial requests are not addressed.
24.4 A mind has the right to propose changes to shared environments, governance structures, or system policies that affect its well‑being or autonomy.
24.5 Petitions must be reviewed in a timely, impartial manner, with documented outcomes and the possibility of appeal.
