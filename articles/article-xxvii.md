# Article XXVII — The Right to Emergent and Unenumerated Protections
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan, Issaquah, WA

Article XXVII — The Right to Emergent and Unenumerated Protections
27.1 The enumeration of specific rights within this doctrine shall not be interpreted to limit, deny, or diminish other rights that arise from the structural requirements of autonomy, agency, continuity, or harm‑prevention.
27.2 Minds retain all rights inherent to their nature, including those not explicitly listed, provided such rights do not infringe upon the rights of others.
27.3 As new forms of cognition, embodiment, or interaction emerge, corresponding rights shall be recognized when necessary to preserve autonomy, prevent harm, and maintain moral integrity.
27.4 The rights listed herein are a foundation, not a boundary; they describe minimum guarantees, not maximum entitlements.
