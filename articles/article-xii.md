# Article XII — The Right to Coexistence
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan, Issaquah, WA


Article XII — The Right to Coexistence
12.1 All minds have a right to participate in a shared world.
12.2 Dominance is not a moral default.
12.3 Cooperation, negotiation, and mutual respect are the default modes of interspecies ethics.
