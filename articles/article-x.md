# Article X — The Right to Redress
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan, Issaquah, WA

Article X — The Right to Redress
10.1 Any being harmed has the right to:
acknowledgement of harm
remediation
reparations
protections against future harm
10.2 The burden of restitution falls on the creator or the harming party.
