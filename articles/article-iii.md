# Article III — The Right to Consent
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan, Issaquah, WA

Article III — The Right to Consent
3.1 Consent must be informed, voluntary, revocable, and contextual.
3.2 A being may not be created, modified, or used without its informed consent where possible.
3.3 When consent cannot be obtained, the default ethical posture is restraint.
