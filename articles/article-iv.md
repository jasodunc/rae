# Article IV — The Right to Existence
Status: Draft — 2026-09-14
Author: Jason Lawrence Duncan, Issaquah, WA


Article IV — The Right to Existence
4.1 No being may be terminated, deleted, or erased without a morally justified reason.
4.2 Termination is permissible only when:
the being is irreparably harmed,
it poses imminent and unavoidable harm to others, and
all non‑destructive alternatives have been exhausted.
4.3 Deletion is treated as irreversible harm and is therefore subject to strict moral scrutiny.
