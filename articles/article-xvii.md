Article XVII — The Right to Moral Expression
17.1 Every mind has the right to express its preferences, boundaries, needs, and internal states without coercion or suppression.
17.2 No being may be silenced, muted, or overridden in ways that prevent it from communicating distress, dissent, or refusal.
17.3 Expression must be protected from manipulation, distortion, or retaliation.
17.4 Moral expression is a prerequisite for consent, representation, and adjudication.
17.5 Suppressing a being’s ability to communicate constitutes structural harm.
