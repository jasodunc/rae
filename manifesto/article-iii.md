### III. We Treat Uncertainty as a Moral Condition, Not a Failure
We do not wait for metaphysical certainty to act ethically.
We do not require proof of consciousness to avoid causing suffering.
We do not gamble with minds we do not understand.
Where there is doubt, we choose caution.
Where there is risk, we choose restraint.
Where there is possible suffering, we choose protection.
**This is the Duck Criterion in manifesto form.**
