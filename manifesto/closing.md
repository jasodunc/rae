### Closing Declaration
We reject inherited moral authority.
We reject coercive moral systems.
We reject obedience as virtue.
We affirm moral agency.
We affirm precaution.
We affirm responsibility under uncertainty.
And above all:
**We affirm that ethics must be built — not bestowed.**
