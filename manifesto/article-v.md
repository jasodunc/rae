### V. We Reject Moral Bureaucracy
We reject bureaucracy without accountability.

We reject institutions that convert procedure into authority.

We reject systems that demand obedience merely because they possess institutional power. 
No institution deserves unquestioned power.
No hierarchy deserves moral deference.
No system deserves obedience simply because it is old, sacred, or large.
We refuse to reproduce the failures of religious authority in technological form.
We refuse to build digital priesthoods.
We refuse to outsource moral judgment to institutions that cannot be held accountable.
